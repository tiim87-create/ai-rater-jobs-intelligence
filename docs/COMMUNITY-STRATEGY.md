# AI Rater Insider — Community Strategy

Approved direction: 2026-11-27. Community is a core strategic pillar and potential long-term value driver, alongside editorial research and market intelligence, not merely a comment feature.

## Product model
- Knowledge & Research: editorial project guides, Operations Atlas, Onboarding Lab.
- Market Intelligence: broad catalog collection with segmented analysis, earnings and task availability.
- Community: public professional discussions, pseudonymous profiles, recurring participation, project experience and user-generated content.
- Contributor Intelligence: structured submissions reviewed by editors and linked to project records. Distinguish testimony, verified evidence and editorial findings.

## MVP proposal
Evaluate wpForo on staging before choosing production software. Start with a small number of boards and vendor/project tags, contextual discussion links, registration, reporting, moderation, subscriptions and clear rules. Public reading and free participation are preferred; premium editorial tools remain separate. Do not request confidential materials, credentials or live exam content. Protect contributor identity and obtain permission for editorial reuse.

## Success measures
Returning active members, unique contributors, meaningful discussions, response rate, moderation burden, accepted structured contributions and editorial knowledge created. Avoid fake engagement and fabricated posts.

## Rollout
Audit existing WordPress infrastructure and contribution entry points; test forum plugin compatibility on staging; define moderation and identity model; seed genuine editorial discussion prompts; integrate project/vendor/job links; launch public beta. No production changes authorized by this strategy document alone.
