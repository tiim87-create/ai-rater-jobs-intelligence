# AI Rater Insider — Ecosystem Strategy

Direction agreed 2026-11-27.

## Mission
The living knowledge ecosystem of the human AI evaluation industry. Document, preserve and understand its evolution through independent research, historical intelligence and collective professional knowledge.

## Architecture
- Historical Intelligence: dated observations of vacancies, pay, workload, projects and operational changes.
- Knowledge Base: Projects, Vendors, Operations Atlas and editorial research.
- Community Space: thematic discussions, Q&A, first-hand experiences, user articles and structured contributions; moderation, pseudonymous profiles, reputation and subscriptions. Community is a core product, not a comments feature.
- Intelligence Tools: Job Intelligence Matrix, ARVI, Earnings and Onboarding Lab.

## History by Design
Preserve dated observations, provenance, coverage, revisions and uncertainty. Distinguish public sources, first-hand evidence, community signals and analysis. Keep historical versions of operational models where lawful and appropriate. Protect personal information, honor deletion requirements and avoid publishing confidential material. Community testimony is not automatically verified fact; editorial review can promote corroborated findings into structured knowledge.

## Status and roadmap
Market Intelligence v1.0 published: four vendor collectors, weekly analytics, ARVI on homepage and Jobs, standalone Market Intelligence page. Browser verification and first scheduled weekly run remain outstanding. Normalized index and richer cross-filters are future enhancements.

Remaining foundation work:
1. Community Space: evaluate WordPress forum infrastructure on staging, registration, moderation, taxonomy, contributor workflows and integration with existing content.
2. Onboarding Lab: complete qualification learning paths and legitimate practice tools, access model and editorial review.
3. Research remaining operational archives for Ads Quality Rating and YouTube/Internet Safety, plus other project folders; inventory before analysis, document evidence and limitations, update project knowledge and Atlas. No archive analysis is claimed complete here.
4. Launch readiness: verify navigation, browser/mobile behavior, data refresh, privacy and moderation policies, backups and access controls. Public site already exists; broader launch means staged product rollout and audience development, not a claim that the site is currently offline.
5. Iteratively develop the resource after launch, prioritizing real user feedback, recurring contributions, historical continuity and reliable data over feature volume.

## Community success
Track returning active participants, meaningful discussions, response rates, accepted contributions and moderation burden. No fabricated engagement. Maintain editorial independence and separate community reports from verified findings.
