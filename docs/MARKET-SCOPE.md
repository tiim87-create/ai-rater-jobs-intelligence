# Market Intelligence scope decision — 2026-11-27

The target market is AI rating and directly related evaluation workflows, not the entire AI contributor or professional outsourcing market.

Exclude domain-specific professional contributor vacancies (finance, legal, medical and other specialist professional domains), including freelance projects, even if they involve AI training. Do not count them in target-market metrics. Preserve raw catalog records for provenance. Vendor membership alone is not evidence of relevance. Explicit search, ads, internet safety, maps and media evaluation roles remain core. Generic AI training, annotation, speech and localization roles require separate scope review; do not silently include them in core metrics. Unknown titles remain unknown, not automatically in scope. Talent pools should be reported separately from active project vacancies.

Implement a separate scope eligibility field and reason, distinct from project family. Recompute historical metrics consistently before publication. This decision does not authorize deleting source records or changing the production vacancy directory.
