# AI Rater Insider — Editorial Attribution Standard

Status: Approved — 2026-10-12
Scope: Knowledge Base, Industry, Projects, and other editorial investigations relying on non-public historical operational records.

## Principle
Keep the focus on the findings, methodology, and their limits. Use restrained attribution to convey that analysis draws on primary operational records without foregrounding the source's identity or chain of custody. Do not imply official endorsement, independently verified authenticity, or current applicability where those have not been established.

## Preferred wording
For general context:
> Based on historical operational data reviewed and analyzed by AI Rater Insider.

For specific findings:
> Our analysis of historical quality-control records indicates that the reporting system distinguishes between blind tests, smoke tests, and golden ratings.

For a short closing note:
> **Research note:** Findings are based on historical operational records analyzed by AI Rater Insider. Certain details have been withheld to protect confidentiality.

## Editorial rules
1. Attribute discreetly, typically once in the introduction or relevant methodological context and once in a brief research note; avoid repeating source descriptions in every paragraph.
2. Distinguish **directly observed fields or records**, **our calculations**, and **interpretations or hypotheses**. Use precise time ranges, denominators, and data limitations for statistics.
3. Do not characterize documents as official, authenticated, leaked, or representative of all vendors or current policy without substantiation.
4. Protect identities and confidential operational details. Do not publish raw source files, personal identifiers, account-level records, or sensitive proprietary thresholds.
5. Before public release, assess permission and applicable confidentiality, privacy, and intellectual-property restrictions. Anonymization and discreet attribution alone do not establish a right to disclose.
6. Maintain detailed provenance and verification notes privately; do not publish confidential third-party material in a public GitHub repository.
7. When source provenance is not independently verified, avoid wording that implies it has been verified.

## Example in article
Historical operational records analyzed by AI Rater Insider contain separate counters for blind tests, smoke tests, and golden ratings. These fields demonstrate a distinction in reporting categories, but do not by themselves establish the exact purpose of each category or whether individual tasks overlap.

*Research note: Findings are based on historical operational records analyzed by AI Rater Insider. Certain details have been withheld to protect confidentiality.*
